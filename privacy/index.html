Privacy Policy

AI Study: Quiz & Flashcards

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This policy explains what information the AI Study: Quiz & Flashcards mobile application ("the app") collects, why it is collected, and the choices you have. The app is developed and published by FR Studio ("we", "us"). By installing or using the app, you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Documents, photos and notes you add stay on your device, except when they are sent for AI processing to create summaries, quizzes, flashcards or exam papers.
  • We may use Google Firebase for analytics and crash reporting.
  • You can delete your content at any time from inside the app.

Information we collect

Content you provide

When you scan a page with the camera, import a PDF, or paste text, that content is stored on your device so the app can show it in your library. To generate a summary, quiz, flashcards, mock exam paper or chat answer, the relevant text from that content may be sent over an encrypted connection to our AI processing provider, used to produce your result, and returned to the app. We do not publish or share your documents with other users.

Camera and files

The camera permission is used only when you actively choose to scan a document. The app does not record in the background. Photos and files are accessed only when you select them, and captured scans may be saved in the app's private storage on your device.

Automatically collected information

We and our service providers may receive limited technical information, such as your device model, operating-system version, app version, language, region, anonymous usage events, and diagnostic information. If the app crashes, an error report containing relevant device and app information may be generated so we can identify and fix the problem.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information included in your message. We retain this information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and provide support.

How we use information

  • To provide features such as scanning, summaries, quizzes, flashcards, exam papers and document chat.
  • To keep your library organised and show your study progress.
  • To diagnose crashes, fix bugs and improve app performance.
  • To protect the app and detect misuse or security problems.
  • To respond to support requests and user feedback.

Third-party services

The app may use the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase — may provide analytics and crash reporting to help us understand app performance and fix technical problems.
  • AI processing provider — processes the text you submit when you request AI-generated summaries, questions, flashcards or answers. Content is transmitted using an encrypted connection.

These providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their handling of information is governed by their applicable privacy policies and data-protection practices.

Data retention and deletion

Documents, scans, quizzes and flashcards remain on your device until you delete them. Deleting an item from the app removes its locally stored copy. Uninstalling the app generally removes information kept in the app's private storage.

Information submitted for AI processing is retained only as required by the processing provider to deliver and protect its service. To request deletion of information associated with you, contact us using the email address below.

Security

Information transmitted between the app and its service providers is protected in transit using encrypted connections. Your local library is stored in the app's private storage area. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

AI-generated content

Summaries, questions, answers and exam papers may be generated automatically and can contain errors or omissions. They are provided as study aids and should be checked against reliable course materials, textbooks or guidance from a teacher.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction or deletion. You may also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing activities. We do not sell personal information. To submit a privacy request, contact us using the email address below.
