Privacy Policy

AI Study: Quiz & Flashcards  · 

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This policy explains what information the AI Study: Quiz & Flashcards mobile application ("the app") collects, why it is collected, and the choices you have. The app is developed and published by FR Studio ("we", "us"). By installing or using the app, you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Documents, photos and notes you add stay on your device, except when they are sent for AI processing to create summaries, quizzes, flashcards or exam papers.
  • We use Google services for ads, payments, analytics and crash reporting.
  • You can delete your content at any time from inside the app.

Information we collect

Content you provide

When you scan a page with the camera, import a PDF, or paste text, that content is stored on your device so the app can show it in your library. To generate a summary, quiz, flashcards, mock exam paper or chat answer, the relevant text from that content is sent over an encrypted connection to our AI processing provider, used to produce your result, and returned to the app. We do not use your documents to train AI models, and we do not publish or share them with other users.

Camera and files

The camera permission is used only when you actively choose to scan a document. The app does not record in the background. Photos and files are read only when you select them, and captured scans are saved in the app's own storage on your device.

Automatically collected information

Like most mobile apps, we and our service providers may receive technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language and region, approximate country-level location derived from IP address, advertising identifier, and anonymous usage events (for example, which screens are opened and whether a feature succeeded or failed). If the app crashes, a diagnostic report containing device state and error details is generated so we can fix the problem.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. We receive confirmation of your purchase status so we can unlock premium features. We never receive or store your card number, bank details or billing address.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we keep it only as long as needed to resolve your issue.

How we use information

  • To provide the core features: scanning, summaries, quizzes, flashcards, exam papers and document chat.
  • To keep your library organised and show your study progress.
  • To operate subscriptions and restore purchases.
  • To display advertising in the free version.
  • To diagnose crashes, fix bugs and improve performance and features.
  • To detect abuse, fraud and violations of our terms.

Third-party services

The app uses the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — advertising in the free version. See the Google advertising policy.
  • Google Play Billing — subscription purchases and renewals.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) — anonymous usage statistics and crash reports.
  • AI processing provider — generates summaries, questions, flashcards and answers from the text you submit. Content is transmitted over an encrypted connection and is not used to train models.

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

Advertising

Ads shown in the free version may use your advertising identifier to limit repeat ads and measure performance. You can reset or delete this identifier at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your Android device, and you can turn off ad personalisation there. Subscribing to any premium plan removes ads.

Data retention and deletion

Documents, scans, quizzes and flashcards remain on your device until you delete them. Deleting an item in the app's library removes it from your device. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored content. Text sent for AI processing is not retained for longer than needed to return your result. To request deletion of any information associated with you, email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

Data sent between the app and our providers is encrypted in transit using TLS. Your library is stored in the app's private storage area, which other apps cannot read. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with such information, contact us and we will delete it.

AI-generated content

Summaries, questions, answers and exam papers are produced automatically and may contain errors or omissions. They are study aids, not authoritative sources, and should be checked against your textbook, course material or teacher. If the app produces content you find offensive or inappropriate, use the report option shown with the generated content, or email us so we can investigate.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete personal information we hold, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Residents of the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom have these rights under the GDPR; residents of California have rights under the CCPA, including the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them. We do not sell personal information as defined by the CCPA. To exercise any right, email us at the address below.
